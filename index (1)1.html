Privacy Policy

GPS · Effective date: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the GPS mobile application (“the App”) handles information when you use it. The App is designed to process information on your device. According to the current information provided by the developer, the App does not operate a developer-controlled backend server.

1. Information and device location

The App may access your device’s location, including precise or approximate location, if you grant the relevant Android permission and use features that require GPS or location services. Location access is used only to provide the App’s location-related functionality, such as displaying or determining your current position.

Location information is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to the developer or stored on a developer-operated server, according to the App’s stated design. You can manage or revoke location permissions in your Android device settings. If you deny permission, some location-based features may not work.

2. Local storage

Information the App saves for its operation or at your direction is stored locally on your device. The developer does not receive or remotely access this locally stored information. The exact information stored depends on the features you use.

3. Collection, sharing and sale of data

Based on the information currently provided by the developer, the App does not send personal information or location information to the developer, does not share such information with third parties, and does not sell user data.

This statement does not cover data independently collected by Android, Google Play services, your device manufacturer, or other applications and services outside the App’s control. If analytics, advertising, crash-reporting tools, maps services, or other third-party SDKs are included in the App, their data practices must be reviewed and this policy updated as necessary.

4. Data retention and deletion

Data stored by the App remains on your device until you delete it. You can generally remove locally stored App data through Android Settings → Apps → GPS → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the App. Clearing data or uninstalling the App may permanently remove information stored locally by the App.

5. Data security

Because the App is designed to keep its data on your device, protection of that data also depends on your device’s security settings and practices. We recommend using a screen lock, keeping Android and the App up to date, and protecting access to your device. No method of electronic storage is guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the App’s actual audience and target age group have not been specified here, the developer should ensure the App’s Google Play target-audience declarations and any applicable Families requirements are accurate.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the App’s features, permissions, data handling, or applicable requirements change. The revised version will be published at the same policy URL with an updated effective date.
